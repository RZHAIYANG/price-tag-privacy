Price Tag stores your products, tags, and settings only on your device. The app has no account system and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

You can delete all app data by deleting the app.

Price Tag is provided as-is for creating and printing price tags.

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription ($6.99 per month or $49.99 per year). Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google account at purchase confirmation.
